# Changelog

All notable changes to **ScriptFlow** by **Naveen Sai** will be documented in this file.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-09
### Initial Release
- **Level 1 (Foundation)**:
  - Smart tab-and-enter block navigation across 7 standard screenplay block types.
  - Live dynamic pagination engine (~54 lines/page standard) with page break dividers.
  - Multi-format import/export: Final Draft (.fdx), Fountain (.fountain), PDF with Title Page, JSON.
  - Dual dialogue side-by-side simultaneous speech.
  - Standard scene numbering (`23`, `23A`, `23B`) on left and right margins.
  - Named revision snapshots with instant rollback.
  - Crash-proof storage queue with Dexie.js IndexedDB and localStorage crash mirror.
- **Level 2 (Writing Experience)**:
  - Typewriter mode with 50% vertical viewport locking.
  - Warm Celluloid night mode for eye fatigue reduction.
  - Fullscreen Zen mode (F11).
  - Scratch Pad drawer for stashing excised lines and dialogue fragments.
  - Corkboard visual index cards and Outline sidebar.
- **Level 3 (AI Intelligence)**:
  - Non-linear narrative causality analyzer (screen time vs chronological story time).
  - Anti-plot-hole validation that respects deliberate twists and flashbacks.
  - 4 beat sheet generators: Save the Cat, Hero's Journey, 3-Act, Story Circle.
  - Anti-formulaic dialogue sparks for writer's block.
- **Level 4 (Production Suite)**:
  - Real-time production breakdown scanner for Cast, Sets, Props, Wardrobe, Vehicles, Stunts.
  - Call sheet generator and exporter with shoot day scheduling and individual call times.
  - 9 WGA revision colors with right margin asterisks.
  - Real-time writers room collaboration simulation with encrypted codes.
- **Level 5 (Delighters)**:
  - Native Web Speech API voice dictation.
  - Visual moodboard for cinematic tone, lighting, and palette curation.
  - Pomodoro sprint timer & word target tracker with progress visualization.
  - Industry screenplay templates for Feature Film, 60m TV Drama, 30m Sitcom, Short Film, and Stage Play.
