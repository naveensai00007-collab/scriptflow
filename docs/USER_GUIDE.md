# ScriptFlow User Guide
**Engineered by Naveen Sai**

Welcome to **ScriptFlow**, the definitive modern screenwriting suite. This guide covers everything you need to know to write, structure, analyze, and produce feature films and television shows.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Creating a Screenplay
- Launch ScriptFlow. On the library home screen, click **"+ New Screenplay"** or choose **"Template Library"** to start with a structured 3-Act Feature, TV Pilot, Sitcom, or Stage Play.
- Click the screenplay title in the top bar anytime to rename your script.
- Click the **Book Icon** in the top bar to edit the **Title Page** (Title, Written By, Logline, Contact Info, and Watermark).

---

## 2. The Writing Engine & Keyboard Flow

ScriptFlow adheres to standard professional screenplay formatting with 12pt Courier Prime typography and exact industry margins:

* **Scene Headings (Sluglines)**: Type `INT.` or `EXT.` to trigger automatic location suggestions (*e.g., INT. COFFEE SHOP - DAY*).
* **Character Names**: Press `Enter` from an Action block and `Tab` to cycle into Character. Type a letter to view character autocomplete from your existing cast.
* **Smart Parentheticals**: Typing `(` while in a Character or Dialogue block automatically formats as a parenthetical direction.
* **Dual Dialogue**: Click the two-column icon next to any Character block to place dialogue side-by-side (for simultaneous speech).
* **Scene Numbers**: Lock or unlock scene numbering (`23`, `23A`) via the Tools menu. Numbers appear on left and right margins of scene headings.

---

## 3. Screenplay Structure Tools

### Corkboard View
- Switch between **Script** and **Corkboard** view in the top bar.
- Each scene heading becomes an index card with synopsis, act grouping, and color flags.
- Reorder scenes with instant drag-and-drop or directional arrows.

### Scratch Pad & Bin
- Click the **Bookmark Icon** in the top bar to open the slide-out **Scratch Pad Drawer**.
- Store excised dialogue lines, alternative twists, and brainstorm notes.
- Click **"Insert to Script"** to drop any snippet back into your current scene.

---

## 4. Anti-AI-Slop Story Intelligence

Click the **AI Studio** button in the top bar:
1. **Beat Sheet Generator**: Choose *Save the Cat (15 beats)*, *Hero's Journey (12 beats)*, *Story Circle (8 beats)*, or *Three-Act*. Enter your premise to receive structural milestones with page target estimates.
2. **Causality & Twist Inspector**: Analyzes your screenplay sequence against chronological time. Validates setups, payoffs, and temporal twists without falsely flagging flashbacks as plot holes.
3. **Anti-Formula Dialogue Sparks**: High-subtext prompts that bypass generic AI tropes.

---

## 5. Production Suite & Call Sheets

### Breakdown View
- Click **"Breakdown"** in the top bar.
- ScriptFlow automatically parses all scenes for Cast, Sets, Props, Wardrobe, Vehicles, and Stunts.
- Filter by category, view scene cross-references, and click **"Export CSV"**.

### Daily Call Sheets
- Click **"Call Sheets"** in the Breakdown view.
- Schedule Shoot Days with crew call time, shooting date, stage location, and individual cast call times.
- Print or save daily call sheets directly.

---

## 6. Revision Drafts & Named Snapshots

- **WGA Revision Colors**: Toggle between 9 industry draft colors (*White, Blue, Pink, Yellow, Green, Goldenrod, Buff, Salmon, Cherry*). Modified blocks receive a margin revision asterisk (`*`).
- **Named Snapshots**: Click the **History Icon** in the top bar to create immutable version snapshots before producer meetings or table reads. Restore anytime with zero data loss.
